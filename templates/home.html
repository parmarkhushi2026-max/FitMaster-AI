Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We're here to help you every step of the way!

Is FitMaster suitable for complete beginners?

add

Yes, absolutely! Every workout plan is tailored to your current fitness level. Whether you are stepping into a gym for the very first time or have years of experience, your workouts start at a comfortable pace and progress naturally.

How do the personalized meal and diet plans work?

add

You receive simple, delicious meal plans built around your dietary preferences (vegetarian, non-veg, vegan, etc.) and daily calorie targets. No extreme starvation or complicated recipes—just wholesome, energizing foods.

Can I workout at home or do I need a gym membership?

add

You can do both! FitMaster offers bodyweight and dumbbell home workout routines as well as comprehensive gym training programs. You choose what fits your schedule best.

How do I get in touch with my assigned trainer?

add

Once you log in, your dedicated trainer's details, schedules, and custom plans are available directly in your dashboard. You can track your progress, request routine adjustments, and get personalized advice anytime.
